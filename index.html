    Serpentine
    How to play
    • Tilt to steer: your phone as a controller, or the phone or tablet you're playing on, like a steering wheel. A little for a gentle curve, more for a tight one. Arrow keys and dragging work too.
    • Jump to clear low lime hurdles: for real while holding a phone controller, or tap anywhere on the screen you're playing on. Space works too.
    • Pink pillars are too tall to jump. Steer around them. From level 5 some slide back and forth.
    • Beat gates rise and fall with the music. Jump them while they're down.
    • Walls can’t be jumped by any snake.
    • Gems make you longer. Don’t run into your tail, but you can jump it.
    • Eat the level’s gem goal to clear it and unlock the next level.
    • You get 3 lives per run. A crash restarts the level, but you keep the gems you'd collected. Clear levels to unlock new snakes.
