      How to play

      • Steer by tilting your phone, or with the arrow keys (A and D work too). A little for a gentle curve, more for a tight one.
      • Jump for real holding a phone controller, tap the screen you're playing on, or press Space. Low obstacles and your own tail can be jumped.
      • Tall obstacles can't be jumped. Steer around them. Some slide back and forth.
      • Beat gates rise and fall with the music. Jump them while they're down.
      • Walls can't be jumped by any snake.
      • Gems make you longer. Eat the level's gem goal to clear it.
      • 3 lives per run. A crash restarts the level, but you keep the gems you'd collected. Clearing levels unlocks new snakes.
